## Focus & Productivity Guard 🛡️

**Focus & Productivity Guard** is a lightweight, high-performance Google Chrome extension designed to help students, professionals, and digital creators reclaim their time and eliminate online distractions. In an era dominated by endless doomscrolling and addictive short-form content, this tool acts as your personal digital shield, keeping you anchored to your core tasks.

### Key Features:

* **Distraction Blocker:** Instantly disables and filters out addictive elements like YouTube Shorts and disruptive social feeds to prevent mindless browsing.
* **Minimalist Popup Interface:** Features a clean, modern, dark-mode UI built with Tailwind CSS for seamless navigation and instant control.
* **Lightweight & Fast:** Engineered for optimal browser performance with zero lag, ensuring your browser runs smoothly while protecting your workflow.
* **Privacy First:** Operates locally with secure Chrome storage, ensuring your browsing habits and settings remain entirely private.

Whether you are preparing for important exams, writing a research paper, or trying to deep-dive into remote work without interruptions, Focus & Productivity Guard provides the friction needed to break bad habits and boost daily output. Install it today and take total control of your digital focus.
